## <img src="assets/screen_advisor_dancer.gif" height="36"> What is this?

Screen Advisor is a little robot that hangs out in a window on your screen and helps you with whatever you're looking at. Quiz, form, chart, error message, a wall of text, whatever. Hit Scan and he reads it and gives you a clean, color-coded answer.

- **Quiz mode:** shows you what's right and what's wrong, even with a bunch of questions on screen. Turn on Auto and it just follows along as you go.
- **Ask mode:** ask your own questions about the screen. Type it or just say it.
- **Presets:** change how it answers. Short and direct, step by step, explained like a tutor, summarized, and more.
- **Privacy first:** personal stuff on your screen (emails, phone numbers, card numbers, etc.) gets blacked out on your own computer before anything gets sent.
- **It only looks:** it never clicks, types, or touches anything for you.

## <img src="assets/screen_advisor_dancer.gif" height="36"> Getting set up

1. Download **ScreenAdvisorSetup.exe** with the green button up top and run it.
2. Windows is probably going to warn you (more on that below). Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Setup handles the rest: downloads the app, makes sure the file checks out, installs it (no admin needed), adds shortcuts, and opens it up.
4. Paste in your **Anthropic API key** when it asks. You can grab one from the [Anthropic Console](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys). Your key stays on your computer.
5. Pick a mode, pick a preset, hit **Scan**. That's it.

## <img src="assets/screen_advisor_dancer.gif" height="36"> Heads-up

- **The "Windows protected your PC" thing:** totally normal. Windows does that for apps that aren't code-signed, which is pretty much every small personal project. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Your antivirus might side-eye it too, for the same reason.
- **It costs a little:** answers come from Claude through your own API key, billed by use, separate from any Claude subscription. Most answers are a fraction of a cent. Research and Think Harder cost more.
- **Screenshots do get sent to Claude:** only after your personal info is blacked out on your PC. The blackout is good, but it's not perfect, so keep **review before sending** turned on, and keep anything super sensitive off your screen.
- **It can be wrong:** it's smart, not perfect. Double-check anything that actually matters.
- **Keep it fair:** follow your school's or work's rules about using AI.

## <img src="assets/screen_advisor_dancer.gif" height="36"> Updates and uninstalling

**Updates are built in.** When there's a new version, a little green dot shows up next to **Help**. Click **Help > Update Available**, then **Update**. Your settings, presets, and key all stay put.

**Want it gone?** Windows **Settings > Apps** ("Apps & features" on Windows 10, "Installed apps" on Windows 11) > **Screen Advisor** > **Uninstall**.
